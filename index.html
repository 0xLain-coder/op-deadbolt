About & Operating Rules

  • No reminders. This console never fires browser notifications or alarms — it only shows the current/next block. Manage alarms externally.
  • Read-only times. Block start/end times come from the FOM v2.1 markdown and cannot be edited in the UI.
  • Hybrid workflow. Draft milestones digitally, finalise on paper, then confirm HIT/MISS at the 20:45 shutdown.
  • Professional modes. Full Capacity · Modified Load · Recovery Plan · Essential Plan.
  • Offline first. Runs as a static site backed by localStorage — works with no network.
